The beginning

Abel was born to Ethiopian immigrants and grew up in Scarborough, a suburb of Toronto. His childhood was not easy: he left school early and struggled with financial difficulties. Music became his escape from reality.

In 2010, Abel uploaded his first songs to YouTube under the pseudonym The Weeknd. His style - a combination of R&B, pop and electronica - instantly caught the attention of listeners.

Since 2015, his career has taken off with the album Beauty Behind the Madness with hits Can't Feel My Face and The Hills.

In addition to music, Abel acts in films and TV series. In 2023, he made his debut as an actor and screenwriter in the series The Idol.

The Idol is a 2023 drama series created by Abel Tesfaye (The Weeknd), Sam Levinson (Euphoria) and Reza Fahim. In the center of the plot is Jocelyn, a pop star trying to return to fame after a nervous breakdown.

The series explores the dark side of the music industry, seduction, power and fame, portraying them through bold, provocative scenes.
